# Trademarks & Attribution

This file explains what the AGPL-3.0 license covers (code) and what it does
not (brand). It is guidance, not legal advice.

## What the license already forces

Under AGPL-3.0 (§4–§5), anyone who forks, sells, or hosts this code must:

- keep all copyright notices intact,
- state that they modified it, with a date,
- license the whole thing under AGPL-3.0 and offer the source to their users.

So forks and sellers **must** carry your copyright and a pointer to the
license. They cannot quietly rebrand the code as 100% their own work.

## Brand rule (trademark, not copyright)

- **"AdopeCanva", the logo (`public/adopecanva.svg`), and associated artwork
  are trademarks of the project.** You may fork, modify, sell, and host the
  code freely — but you may not present your fork as AdopeCanva, imply
  endorsement, or use the name/logo in a confusingly similar product.
- Rebrands must pick their own name and mark. A credit line such as
  *"Based on AdopeCanva (AGPL-3.0) — https://github.com/rjarivi/ADopeCanva"*
  is appreciated but voluntary.
- This site ships with an open-source footer credit linking to the repo and
  adopecanva.com. Removing it from your fork is allowed by the license, but
  keeping it is good citizenship — and keeps you a friend of the project.

## For contributors

Keep existing copyright headers intact, don't strip attribution, and don't
submit third-party assets (fonts, icons, images) you don't have rights to.
